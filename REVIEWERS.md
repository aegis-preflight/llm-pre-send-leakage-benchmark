## Anonymous cross-check contributions

Anonymous reviewers who declined naming are thanked collectively in
`paper/paper.md` `§9 Acknowledgments` without individual attribution.

## How this file updates

- Reviewers who complete a review and opt in: add a row above with
  their preferred name, affiliation, review scope, and opt-in date.
- Reviewers who withdraw: remove the row.
- Reviewers who complete review but decline naming: no row is added;
  their contribution is credited anonymously in the paper.

This file is intended to stay small and readable. If reviewer count
grows past what fits in a single markdown table, split by version
(one section per released tag).
